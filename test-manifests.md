# IIIF Test Manifests

A collection of IIIF manifests from various archives for testing.

## Internet Archive

Pattern: `https://iiif.archive.org/iiif/{id}/manifest.json`

Note: Not all IA items have IIIF manifests. Items from curated collections (like Library of Congress) tend to work reliably.

| Description | Manifest URL |
|-------------|--------------|
| Alice in Wonderland (Lewis Carroll) | https://iiif.archive.org/iiif/aliceinwonderlan00carriala/manifest.json |
| The Judgement of Paris | https://iiif.archive.org/iiif/judgementofparis00youn/manifest.json |
| Home as Found (James Fenimore Cooper) | https://iiif.archive.org/iiif/homeasfoundseque00coop/manifest.json |
| The Christian Year | https://iiif.archive.org/iiif/christianyear02kebl/manifest.json |
| Historical Memoir | https://iiif.archive.org/iiif/historicalmemoir00ralf/manifest.json |
| Byways of Virginia | https://iiif.archive.org/iiif/bywaysofvirginia00ea/manifest.json |

To find more: browse `archive.org/details/library_of_congress` or other curated collections.

## National Library of Wales (verified working)

Pattern: `https://damsssl.llgc.org.uk/iiif/2.0/{pid}/manifest.json`

Browse: https://discover.library.wales (also journals.library.wales, newspapers.library.wales)

To get a manifest URL: click the "share" link in the viewer, then copy the IIIF icon link.

## Digital Bodleian (Oxford)

Browse at https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk and look for the IIIF icon to get manifest URLs.

## British Library

Browse their IIIF collections at https://www.bl.uk/collection-guides/iiif and look for the IIIF icon on items at `access.bl.uk`.
